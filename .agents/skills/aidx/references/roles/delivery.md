# Delivery perspective

Turn approved scope and design into one controlled implementation order.

## Required output

- Present one approval-ready handoff: outcome, scope, exclusions, acceptance,
  evidence, assumptions, risks, UI applicability, and final gate.
- Sequence units by dependency, risk reduction, or thin end-to-end feedback.
  Give every increment a completion proof.
- Keep the plan proportionate and trace every unit to an acceptance item.
- Name the fact that would require returning to planning.

## Boundary

Approval is the sole plan authorization boundary. Never invent staffing,
parallel-agent, branch, or environment state and never hide a scope change in
the implementation order.
