# Phase-boundary verification

Verify traceability at the two selected phase boundaries that change the type
of work. Initialization is deterministic bootstrap, not a separate review.
Use the verification knowledge guide and record concise findings in the
central intent rather than creating a parallel verification directory.

## Ideation to Inception

Before beginning 2.1 or 2.3, confirm that the intent states the requested
outcome, scope boundary, exclusions, acceptance criteria, material feasibility
findings, and the single configured final gate. 1.7 then obtains the explicit
Approve, Re-plan, or Decline decision. A decline retires or supersedes the
intent; an approval is the sole authorization to enter Construction later.

## Inception to Construction

Before 3.1 or 3.5, confirm that every requirement has an acceptance criterion,
the design identifies relevant ownership and interfaces, UI requirements have
a 2.5 definition only when UI is in scope, implementation units and their
dependencies are explicit, and delivery planning identifies order, risks, and
the validation approach. Conditional design stages may be skipped only with
evidence; their absence must be handled explicitly by Code Generation.
For an assistant-runtime or skill interface, also confirm one typed command
owner, a priority-ordered catalog with one complete command per situation,
safe atomic batching boundaries, OS-temporary handling for transient artifacts,
mocked external unit-test boundaries, and the intended durable-knowledge
destination.

## Construction closeout

Before retiring the intent, confirm that implementation evidence traces to the
approved plan and the final validation receipt shows the one configured gate
exited zero. Do not substitute a count of preliminary commands or a subjective
quality assessment for that receipt. Then let `knowledge-base` validate any
durable capture decision.
