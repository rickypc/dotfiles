# Knowledge Distillation

Capture only validated durable facts, decisions, or observed lessons through
knowledge-base. Do not retain raw chat, transient progress, or sensitive data.
After the KB concept and its indexes are verified, complete this terminal stage
and retire the single temporary intent with the private KB root and every
captured concept path. Never retire an active or undistilled intent. If no
durable knowledge exists, skip this stage with its factual reason before
retiring the intent without KB arguments.
